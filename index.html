ALLM-1: Adaptive Lite Language Model

Fine-tunes DistilBERT and T5-small, quantizes them to INT8 and ONNX uint8 and benchmarks them on CPU. The uint8 classifier is 4× smaller and 2.1× faster at 91.8% accuracy (vs 91.7% FP32). Served via FastAPI from a versioned model registry with an LRU model cache.

Measures how a credit-risk model decays on Lending Club data (2015–2018), detects drift with PSI, KS, Jensen-Shannon and a classifier two-sample test, and shows adaptive retraining recovers up to +0.053 AUC. MLflow, DVC, SHAP stability and a Streamlit dashboard.
